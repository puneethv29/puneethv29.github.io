How to export from NASDAQ screener:
  1. Go to nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/screener
  2. Set your filters (exchange, market cap, etc.)
  3. Click Download CSV at the bottom of the results
  4. Drop that file here

The CSV should have columns: Symbol, Name, Last Sale, Market Cap, Volume, Sector, Industry. Current prices and sector/industry will be imported instantly. 6-month prices will then be fetched from Yahoo Finance in the background, sorted by market cap so large-caps appear first.
